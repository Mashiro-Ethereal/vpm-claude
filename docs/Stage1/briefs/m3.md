# Specification: PickCube-v1 reference candidate

The reference candidate is the human-written answer for PickCube-v1. It is a test fixture for the first layer on the real environment and the answer that the PickCube trial run is compared with. It is never put into an input package. It follows candidate_api.md and the design rules exactly, in the style of the example candidate (vpmkit/resources/example/candidate/).

Location: `candidates/reference/PickCube-v1/` with `stage_graph.yaml`, `bindings.py`, `predicates.py`, `progress.py`, `notes.md`.

## Source facts (ManiSkill 3.0.1, mani_skill/envs/tasks/tabletop/pick_cube.py, robot panda)

- `evaluate()`:
  - `is_obj_placed = torch.linalg.norm(self.goal_site.pose.p - self.cube.pose.p, axis=1) <= self.goal_thresh`
  - `is_grasped = self.agent.is_grasping(self.cube)` (Panda.is_grasping, default min_force=0.5, max_angle=85)
  - `is_robot_static = self.agent.is_static(0.2)`; Panda.is_static: `qvel = self.robot.get_qvel()[..., :-2]; return torch.max(torch.abs(qvel), 1)[0] <= threshold`
  - `success = is_obj_placed & is_robot_static`
- For panda (PICK_CUBE_CONFIGS["panda"]): `cube_half_size = 0.02` (a Python float), `goal_thresh = 0.025`, spawn region ±0.1 m in x and y, goal height `cube_half_size + U[0, 0.3]`.
- `goal_site` is a kinematic sphere of radius goal_thresh without collision, registered in `_hidden_objects`; `Actor.pose` returns its true pose even while it is hidden.

## bindings.py

```python
"""Object bindings for PickCube-v1 (reference candidate)."""


def bind(env, api):
    # cube_half_size is the attribute the scene uses to build the cube.
    # The goal is a target point, bound as a site.
    return {
        "g": api.gripper(),
        "cube": api.actor(env.cube, half_size=env.cube_half_size),
        "goal": api.site(lambda env: env.goal_site.pose),
    }
```

## predicates.py

Measurement function `tcp_dist(w, g, o)`: distance (m) from the TCP of g to the center of o, as in the example.

| Predicate | Definition (margin) | Unit | Official | scale | need_true / need_false |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `near(w, g, o, max_dist=0.02)` | ‖o.half_size‖ + max_dist − tcp_dist(w, g, o) | m | no | 0.3 | "{g} is far from {o}; move {g} to {o}." / "{g} is too close to {o}; move {g} away from {o}." |
| `grasped(w, g, o)` | signed(w.grasping(g, o)) | ±1 | is_grasped | 1.0 | "{o} is not held; grasp {o} with {g}." / "{o} is still held; release {o}." |
| `at_goal(w, o, t, max_dist=0.025)` | max_dist − ‖t.pos − o.pos‖ (3-D) | m | is_obj_placed | 0.3 | "{o} is not at {t}; move {o} to {t}." / "{o} is at {t}; move {o} away from {t}." |
| `arm_static(w, g, max_qvel=0.2)` | max_qvel − max over joints of \|g.arm_qvel\| | rad/s | is_robot_static | 1.0 | "{g} is still moving; hold {g} still." / "{g} is at rest; move {g}." |

desc strings (one sentence each, matching the code):
- near: "The TCP of g is within max_dist of the bounding sphere of o: its distance to the center of o is at most o's half diagonal plus max_dist." (same as the example)
- grasped: "The gripper g holds o: both fingers press on o (the adapter's grasp reading)." (same as the example)
- at_goal: "The center of o is within max_dist of the position of t (3-D Euclidean distance)."
- arm_static: "The arm of g is at rest: every arm joint, fingers excluded, moves at most max_qvel rad/s."

Comments next to the official predicates say what they copy:
- at_goal: same test as is_obj_placed in evaluate(): ‖goal_site.p − cube.p‖ ≤ goal_thresh = 0.025 (the panda value).
- arm_static: same test as Panda.is_static(0.2) in evaluate(): g.arm_qvel is the robot's qvel without the two finger joints.

## progress.py

```python
"""Progress functions for PickCube-v1 (reference candidate).

Only reach has its own progress. grasp has a boolean guard and gets none;
carry and settle use the default progress of their guard literals.
"""
from .predicates import tcp_dist


def progress_reach(w, o, d0=0.3):
    # 1 at the cube's center, 0 at d0 or farther. d0 is a fixed scale declared
    # in params, not the distance at which the stage began.
    return 1.0 - tcp_dist(w, o.g, o.cube) / d0
```

## notes.md (the four headings of candidate_api.md section 10)

1. Why the stages are split this way:
   - One pick-and-place round without the release: reach → grasp → carry → settle. Boundaries sit where contact changes (the fingers close on the cube) and where goal relations are reached (the cube is at the goal; the arm is at rest). PickCube's success does not require letting go, so there is no release stage.
   - reach only brings the gripper to the cube's vicinity (`near`); lining up and closing are left to exploration during grasp.
   - Invariants: grasp keeps `near(g, cube)` → reach (the gripper wanders off while trying to grasp); carry keeps `grasped(g, cube)` → reach (the cube slips out; the teacher walks forward again in the same step: to grasp if the gripper is still at the cube, else reach); settle keeps `at_goal(cube, goal)` → carry, the stage that established it (the cube drifts out of the goal while the arm slows down). If the cube was also dropped, carry's invariant sends the teacher to reach at the next step; there is one step of carry guidance for a cube that is no longer held.
   - Progress: reach has its own progress 1 − d/d0 with the fixed scale `progress.reach.d0`; grasp has a boolean guard and none; carry and settle use the default progress (at_goal with scale 0.3 m; arm_static with scale 1 rad/s).
   - Other ways: when the goal is low (its height is the cube's resting height plus U[0, 0.3]), pushing the cube to the goal without grasping can also succeed. The chain does not follow this.
2. Guards with more than one literal: none.
3. Predicates rewritten from evaluate(): table with grasped ↔ is_grasped (w.grasping is agent.is_grasping with its default arguments, the call evaluate() makes); at_goal ↔ is_obj_placed (3-D distance between the goal's position and the cube's center ≤ goal_thresh = 0.025, the panda value); arm_static ↔ is_robot_static (max |qvel| over the arm joints ≤ 0.2; arm_qvel excludes the two finger joints, as Panda.is_static does); arm_static and at_goal ↔ success (settle's guard plus its invariant). Their thresholds are fixed and not in params; near's threshold is tunable.
4. Uncertainties:
   - near.max_dist = 0.02: near holds within about 5.5 cm of the cube's center (half diagonal 3.46 cm plus 2 cm).
   - goal_thresh is the panda value; another robot would need another value.
   - arm_static can flicker while the arm decelerates; at_goal can flicker at the 2.5 cm boundary, moving the teacher between carry and settle.
   - grasped comes from contact forces and can flicker in the first steps of a grasp; each flicker during carry sends the teacher back to reach, and it walks forward again in the same step.
